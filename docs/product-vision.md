# Uber Genius - Product Vision

## Vision

Build the best analytics and insight platform for Uber drivers.

The platform will help drivers understand their earnings, identify profitable strategies, and make data-driven decisions that are impossible using the standard Uber Driver app.

The long-term goal is to become the definitive platform for understanding how to maximize earnings on Uber through data, visualization, and experimentation.

---

## The Problem

The Uber Driver app provides only basic summaries of earnings and trips.

Drivers have many unanswered questions:

- Which neighborhoods actually make the most money?
- What hours consistently produce the highest hourly income?
- Which trip types are most profitable?
- Which requests should I accept or decline?
- What happens if I only accept airport trips?
- How much do promotions really increase earnings?
- How much time do I spend driving unpaid miles?

Drivers often rely on intuition, YouTube advice, Reddit discussions, or spreadsheets because Uber does not provide the level of insight they need.

---

## The Solution

Create a web application where drivers can upload their Uber trip history and instantly receive advanced analytics.

Examples include:

- Interactive maps
- Earnings heat maps
- Hourly income charts
- Acceptance rate analysis
- Deadhead (unpaid) mileage estimates
- Pickup and drop-off trends
- Airport performance analysis
- Surge analysis
- Tip analysis
- Trip profitability analysis
- Weekly and monthly trends

Eventually, the platform will recommend better driving strategies using historical data and personalized insights.

---

## Guiding Principles

- **Data first**
- **Beautiful visualizations**
- **Fast and intuitive**
- **Research focused**
- **Privacy focused**
- **Drivers always own their data**
- **Every feature should answer an important business question**

---

## Product Roadmap

### Phase 1 – Web Analytics

Build a web application where drivers upload their Uber trip history and explore rich analytics that go far beyond what the Uber Driver app provides.

---

### Phase 2 – Intelligent Insights

Use analytics from uploaded trip history to move beyond dashboards and provide personalized recommendations.

The platform begins answering questions such as:

- "Which areas consistently earn me the most?"
- "What times of day produce my highest hourly earnings?"
- "Which trip types are most profitable for me?"
- "How much time am I spending on unpaid miles?"
- "Should I accept rides farther than 10 minutes away based on my historical results?"
- "What changes could increase my hourly income?"

The focus is creating an intelligent assistant that learns from each driver's unique history and helps them make better decisions.

---

### Phase 3 – Mobile Companion

Develop a mobile app that can record trips in real time with the driver's permission.

By collecting GPS location, timestamps, movement, and other contextual data during each trip, the platform can measure details that Uber's exported reports do not include:

- Actual routes taken
- True unpaid driving distance
- Waiting time between rides
- Traffic impacts
- Location-based trends
- Driving patterns

This richer data allows the platform to provide more accurate recommendations and eventually move from analyzing past performance to helping drivers optimize future decisions.

---

## Initial Product Goals

The first release should focus on simplicity.

Users can:

- Upload Uber trip history
- View a personal analytics dashboard
- Filter trips by date
- Explore interactive maps
- See earnings breakdowns
- Compare driving sessions
- Save historical uploads

No recommendations are required initially.

The first objective is simply presenting Uber data better than the Uber Driver app.

---

## Long-Term Vision

As the platform grows, it evolves from an analytics dashboard into a comprehensive research and decision-support platform for rideshare optimization.

The goal is to help drivers move beyond understanding past performance and toward making better decisions about where, when, and how they drive.

Future capabilities may include:

- Simulating different driving strategies before committing to them
- Identifying patterns across locations, times, and trip types
- Comparing personal performance against historical trends
- Providing personalized recommendations based on individual driving history
- Tracking real-world driving behavior through optional mobile data collection
- Helping drivers evaluate the potential value of different ride opportunities
- Building a deeper understanding of what factors contribute to profitable driving

Ultimately, the platform should help drivers answer one question before every decision:

> "What is the most profitable thing I can do next?"
